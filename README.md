## 👋 About me

I'm a **Computer Science student at UT San Antonio** with a focus on **cybersecurity and network engineering**. I like understanding how systems work from the wire up, which is why I spend my time competing in cyber defense events, running a homelab, and taking apart consoles to see what they can really do.

- 🛡️ Competed in **CCDC / NCCDC**, defending live networks against a red team
- 🏠 Run a self-hosted **Raspberry Pi 5 server** with a Dockerized, Tailscale-only stack
- 🎮 Write **homebrew for the Nintendo 3DS** and mod games for fun
- 🤖 Currently teaching a **reinforcement-learning agent** to play Balatro
- 🌱 Looking for internships and open-source projects where I can learn from people ahead of me
